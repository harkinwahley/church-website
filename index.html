About Our Ministry

The Way of God Bible Ministry, also known as Mountain of Praise, was founded on the divine mandate to spread the unadulterated Word of God and raise disciples for Christ. We have been a beacon of hope and transformation in our community, fostering spiritual growth, nurturing families, and impacting lives through the power of God's love.

Our journey has been one of faith, dedication, and countless testimonies. Join us as we walk in the path of righteousness, experiencing God's miraculous hand in every aspect of our lives.
